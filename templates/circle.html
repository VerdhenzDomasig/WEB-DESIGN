Area of Circle

○

Area = π × radius²

Result Your result will appear here.
A QUICK GUIDE

Basic information

The area of a circle measures the surface enclosed by its circumference. The radius runs from the center to the boundary. [1]

Formula and terms

A=πr2
  • r: radius.
  • d = 2r: diameter.
  • π ≈ 3.14159: the circle constant.
  • A: area in square units.

Worked example

Given: r = 3 cm.

A=π32=9π≈28.27 cm²

How to use the calculator

  1. Find the radius. If you know the diameter, halve it first.
  2. Enter the radius and click Calculate Area.
  3. Use the square of the measurement unit for the answer.

Applications and input rules

Useful for circular gardens, tabletops, and design layouts.

The calculation uses JavaScript’s Math.PI. [2] This demo accepts finite, non-negative radii and decimals; a radius of zero returns zero area.
