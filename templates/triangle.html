Area of Triangle

△

Area = ½ × base × height

Result Your result will appear here.
A QUICK GUIDE

Basic information

The area of a triangle measures the surface inside its boundary. It depends on a chosen base and the perpendicular height. [1]

Formula and terms

A=b×h2
  • b: the chosen base length.
  • h: height measured at 90° to that base.
  • A: area in square units.

Worked example

Given: b = 10 cm, h = 4 cm.

A=10×42=20 cm²

How to use the calculator

  1. Use the same unit for base and height.
  2. Enter both measurements.
  3. Click Calculate Area.

If measurements are in centimeters, the result is in square centimeters.

[2]

Applications and input rules

Useful for estimating triangular floor spaces, panels, and designs.

This calculator accepts finite, non-negative lengths and decimals. A zero base or height returns zero area.
